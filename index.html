A controlled route to production

From uncertainty to controlled production

Each gate converts uncertainty into evidence. Funding and commitment increase only when the product is ready for the next stage.

  1. 01
    DEFINE

    Product & risk brief

    Requirements, constraints, volumes, target cost, timeline and unknowns.

  2. 02
    DE-RISK

    DFM & supplier fit

    Process choice, feasibility, quotes, lead times and technical challenge.

  3. 03
    PROVE

    Prototype & validation

    Test intent, design iterations, critical dimensions and acceptance criteria.

  4. 04
    CONTROL

    Tooling & pilot

    Build readiness, quality controls, yield, packaging and corrective actions.

  5. 05
    SCALE

    Production release

    Evidence-based approval, OQC, shipment readiness and continuous improvement.
